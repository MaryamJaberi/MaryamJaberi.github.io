Announcement to Staff

Template message that management sends to employees. Feel free to adapt it to your own tone.

Sample text. This is a draft we finalise with you before the pilot; it is not legal advice. Text in [brackets] is filled in by you.

Subject

Take part in our anonymous wellbeing check (about 15 minutes)

Message

Dear colleagues,

We want to understand better how you are doing at work, so we can invest in what really helps. That's why, from [date] to [date], we are taking part in the Wellbeing Check by SoloNovate.

  • Anonymous: you won't be asked for your name. As management we only see group results of at least 5 people, never what you answer.
  • Voluntary: taking part or not has no consequences at all.
  • Short: 8 to 18 minutes, on your phone or computer, in English, Dutch or Persian.
  • For you too: when you finish, you immediately see your own result with two practical tips. Only you see it.

What will we do with it? We will discuss the results [with the works council and] in the management team, and before [date] share a short summary with everyone, together with the steps we will take.
